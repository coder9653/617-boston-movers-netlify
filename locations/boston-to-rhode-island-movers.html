Life in Rhode Island

Why Move From Boston To Rhode Island?

A Different Everyday Vibe

Rhode Island has a great mix of city neighbourhoods, suburbs and coastal towns. Depending on where you settle, you might find the pace of life quite different from Boston.

Housing & Living Costs

Housing costs and everyday expenses vary between Rhode Island communities. Comparing rent, property prices, utilities and transport costs can help you see how your budget might change after the move.

Easy Access To Boston & Beyond

Rhode Island's right next door to Massachusetts, so getting back and forth for work, family visits and other plans is totally doable. How long it takes depends on where you live and how you travel.

Beaches & Outdoor Fun

The state's known for its coastline, beaches and outdoor attractions. Places like Narragansett Beach, Misquamicut State Beach and East Matunuck State Beach draw visitors all year round.

Great Food & Culture

Rhode Island's got a brilliant range of restaurants, local markets, museums, theatres and cultural spots. Exploring your new community is a great way to get to know the area's food, history and local life.

Moving To Rhode Island: Your Questions

How long does a move from Boston to Rhode Island take?

It depends on where you're going, the size of your place, how much you're bringing and the services you need. Pickup, loading, travel and unloading all add up. Send us your addresses and we'll chat about the schedule.

Do I need to be there for the whole move?

It's really helpful to have someone around to let us in and pass on any important instructions at pickup and delivery. If you can't be there the whole time, just chat to the team about other arrangements beforehand.

What can't professional movers move?

Some hazardous, flammable or restricted items might not be suitable for transport. Rules vary, so ask us about anything you're unsure of before you pack it.

Do you offer packing and unpacking?

Yes, we can chat about packing help when you arrange your move. Let us know which belongings need packing and how much help you'd like.

Can you protect furniture during the move?

Definitely — we use protective moving blankets to help reduce scratches and marks while we handle and transport furniture. Let us know about any delicate or valuable pieces before moving day.

Can you take apart and rebuild beds and dressers?

Yes, we can take apart and rebuild selected pieces. Just point out which ones need a hand when we chat about your move.

Can you help rearrange furniture at my new home?

Of course! We can chat about placing and rearranging furniture as part of your move. Just tell us where you'd like the bigger pieces to go.
