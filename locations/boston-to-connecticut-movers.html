Before you move

Getting Ready For Your New Life In Connecticut

Moving to another state is a great chance to plan ahead. As well as booking your movers, have a think about anything that could affect your move and your first few weeks in Connecticut.

Give Yourself A Realistic Timeline

How long your move takes depends on the size of your place, the distance, the services you need and the delivery plan. Lock in your dates early and leave plenty of time for packing, prep and sorting access.

Check Out Living Costs

Living costs can vary quite a bit across Connecticut. Housing, utilities, transport and everyday spending might be quite different between bigger cities, coastal towns and quieter communities. Do some research on where you're settling so you can budget properly.

Get Ready For The Seasons

Connecticut has cold winters, snow in some places and warm summers. Weather can affect travel and moving plans — especially in winter — so check the forecast before moving day and keep your essentials close by.
