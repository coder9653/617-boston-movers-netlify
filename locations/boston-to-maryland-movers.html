Before moving day

A Few Handy Checks Before Moving Day

Moving between Boston and Maryland can mean different property layouts, building rules and access set-ups. Sorting a few practical bits early makes pickup and delivery so much smoother.

Check Access & Parking

Some Boston buildings have narrow stairs, shared entrances, hardly any street parking or set moving hours. Maryland apartments and communities can have rules for delivery trucks too.

Have a word with both property managers about access, parking, loading areas and any bookings you need.

Have A Clear-Out First

Decide which furniture, appliances, boxes and personal items are making the trip. A tidy inventory helps us size your move and plan the right set-up.

Put aside anything you're donating, selling or throwing away before moving day.

Mention The Fragile & Oversized Stuff

Artwork, mirrors, glass furniture, big appliances and delicate items may need extra prep. Let us know about them when you ask for an estimate so we can plan ahead.

Line Up Your Dates

If you're renting or buying, check when you can actually get into the new place. Plan around lease dates, closing schedules, picking up keys and any delivery-time limits.

Keep The Essentials Close

Where it makes sense, keep important documents, daily meds, valuables, chargers and other must-haves separate from the main load. It'll make your first few days much easier.
