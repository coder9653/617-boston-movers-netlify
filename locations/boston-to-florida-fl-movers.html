It's A Long Way — So Planning Matters

When you're moving from Massachusetts to Florida, both ends of the trip need preparing. Your furniture, boxes, appliances and personal bits all have to be packed and secured for a lot of miles.

A surprising amount happens before the truck even shows up: sorting out building access, grabbing a parking spot, getting packing supplies, prepping the big furniture and agreeing a delivery time that suits your new home or office.

We approach every interstate move the same way — plan first, then coordinate. We'll help with each part of it, from protecting your things and loading the truck to driving them down and arranging them when they arrive.

Studio apartment? Big family house? Business premises? We do them all. Whether it's a few chunky pieces of furniture or everything you own, we'll build the plan around your shipment.

Moving services

How We Can Help With Your Florida Move

Everyone's move looks a little different. Some folks just want a hand with packing and getting furniture ready; others would rather we took care of the lot — loading, driving, unloading and putting furniture where it goes.

Moving Your Household

Moving house to another state usually means a lot of stuff: the sofa and beds, kitchen gear, the TV and computers, artwork and the fragile things you really don't want broken.

We move apartments, condos and houses of every size. We'll get your furniture ready for the road, protect anything delicate and load the truck so everything's secure for the long haul.

Leaving a studio or a five-bedroom house, we'll talk through exactly what you need.

Moving Your Business

If your company's relocating from Boston to Florida, you'll need a plan for furniture, office equipment, supplies and access at both buildings.

We help offices and businesses prepare their furniture, organise equipment for the trip and coordinate loading and delivery to fit around how you work.

A well-organised move makes it much easier for your team to switch locations and get the new space up and running.

Packing & Furniture Protection

Packing for a long-distance move needs the right materials and a careful touch. Something that's fine for a quick trip across town might need a lot more protection to survive a drive this long.

We'll wrap furniture and fragile pieces in moving blankets, shrink wrap, mattress covers and whatever else they need to stay safe during loading and transport.

We can also take furniture apart and prep it, so the big pieces are easier to handle and load securely.

Packing

Our professional packers at 617 Boston Movers use good-quality materials to keep your belongings secure. We get how much your things matter, so we treat everything from delicate glassware to heavy furniture with care and attention to detail. We've got the techniques to wrap, cushion and box your items so there's less chance of anything getting damaged on the way. Hand the packing over to us and you'll save time and stress, knowing your stuff's in good hands — it's a complete packing service focused on keeping everything safe from start to finish.

Taking Furniture Apart & Putting It Back

Big furniture sometimes has to be dismantled before it can travel. Beds, tables, wardrobes and other bulky pieces can be a real struggle in narrow halls, stairwells and doorways otherwise.

We'll take suitable pieces apart before loading and rebuild them once they're delivered, which makes moving day easier and means everything gets set up properly in your new home.

How it works

Here's How Your Boston-To-Florida Move Works

A smooth interstate move really comes down to getting each stage right. A clear process — from our first chat to the last piece of furniture going in place — keeps confusion and delays to a minimum.

  1. 01

    Tell Us What You've Got

    Start by letting us know about your Boston place, where you're heading in Florida, how big the move is and what help you'd like.

    That gives us the scope and tells us what needs arranging.

  2. 02

    Get Your Stuff Ready

    Before moving day, work out what's coming with you. If you want help packing, we'll sort the materials and prep.

    We'll also flag any furniture that needs taking apart before it's loaded.

  3. 03

    Protect The Precious Things

    Furniture and delicate belongings get proper protection before they go on the truck.

    We use protective materials and careful handling to help cut the risk of scratches, dents and damage on the road.

  4. 04

    Load It Up Properly

    Loading is all about arranging things carefully and making the most of the space.

    Heavy furniture, boxes and fragile items are positioned and secured with the long trip in mind.

  5. 05

    Drive It Down South

    Your belongings head from the Boston area to wherever you're going in Florida.

    Good route planning, clear communication and coordinated delivery are what make a long-distance move work.

  6. 06

    Unload & Make It Home

    When we arrive, we unload, put furniture in the rooms you want and rebuild anything that was taken apart.

    We can also do a post-move clean-up and take away packing materials and moving rubbish.
